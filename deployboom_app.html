Nonlinear SMA Constitutive Dynamics & Phase Hysteresis Sentinel

Coiled shape-memory alloy (SMA) boom actuators undergo sub-surface martensite-to-austenite shear transformations. In deep-space thermal gradients (e.g. shadowed planetary occultations at 80 K), partial phase crystallization causes mechanical stalls before physical micro-latching. DeployBoom continuously monitors the dynamic $(F, \theta)$ trajectory to invert and eliminate phase locks.

1. SMA Macroscopic Thermomechanical Law (Brinson-Tanaka)

The internal mechanical stress $\sigma$ and resultant boom recovery deployment force $F(t)$ are governed by elastic modulus $D(\xi)$, mechanical strain $\varepsilon$, transformation tensor $\Omega(\xi)$, and thermal expansion coefficient $\Theta$:

$$\sigma - \sigma_0 = D(\xi)(\varepsilon - \varepsilon_0) + \Omega(\xi)(\xi_S - \xi_{S0}) + \Theta(T - T_0)$$

Where total martensite fraction $\xi = \xi_S + \xi_T$ decouples into stress-induced detwinned martensite $\xi_S$ and temperature-induced twinned martensite $\xi_T$. The net recovery force driving the composite coiled slit-tube boom is:

$$F_{\text{recovery}}(t) = \kappa_{\text{geom}} \int_{A} \sigma(r, \theta_{\text{SMA}}, T) \, dA - F_{\text{friction}}(\dot{x})$$
Nonlinear geometric stiffness coefficient: $\kappa_{\text{geom}} = \frac{E I_{\text{slit}}}{L_{\text{boom}}^2 (1 - \nu^2)}$
2. Crystallographic Phase Fraction & Thermal Shadow Stalls

As resistive current passes through the embedded NiTiNOL trigger wire, conversion from monoclinic B19' (martensite) to cubic B2 (austenite) commences at $A_s$ (Austenite Start) and concludes at $A_f$ (Austenite Finish) under forward kinetics:

$$\xi = \frac{\xi_0}{2} \left\{ \cos\left[ \frac{\pi}{A_f - A_s}\left( T - A_s - \frac{\sigma}{C_A} \right) \right] + 1 \right\}$$

Deep space radiative losses ($q_{\text{rad}} = \epsilon \sigma_{\text{SB}} (T^4 - T_{\text{space}}^4)$) inside penumbral shadows create local thermal pin-down where $T < A_s + \sigma / C_A$. At this boundary, phase conversion freezes at intermediate $\theta^*$, halting force generation below the latching threshold.

$$\theta_{\text{phase}} = \arccos(2\xi - 1) \quad \text{such that} \quad \left.\frac{d\theta}{dt}\right|_{\text{stall}} \to 0 \quad (\sigma < \sigma_{\text{latch}})$$
Phase stress influence coefficient: $C_A \approx 8.2\text{ MPa/K}$, $A_f = 345\text{ K}$, $A_s = 312\text{ K}$.
3. Dynamic Hysteresis Observer & Anomaly Detection

Embedded magnetic permeability sensors measure crystalline susceptibility ($\chi_m(t) \propto 1 - \xi$) while fiber Bragg grating (FBG) arrays measure force-induced strain $\varepsilon_{\text{FBG}}$. The flight sentinel computes phase coordinate $\mathbf{x} = [\theta, F]^T$:

$$\mathcal{H}(\theta, F) = F - \left[ F_{\text{min}} + (F_{\text{latch}} - F_{\text{min}}) \frac{1}{1 + e^{-\beta(\theta - \theta_{\text{mid}})}} \right]$$

The Lyapunov-stable fault condition triggers when phase velocity vanishes outside the nominal boundary:

$$\Psi_{\text{anomaly}} = \mathbb{I}\left( \left|\frac{d\theta}{dt}\right| < \epsilon_{\theta} \;\land\; F(t) < F_{\text{latch\_min}} \;\land\; t_{\text{heat}} > \tau_{\text{crit}} \right)$$
Observer execution rate: 25 Hz deterministic cyclic interrupt with zero float division overhead.
4. Substrate Coiling & Secondary Current Injection

When $\Psi_{\text{anomaly}} = 1$, the flight FPGA re-routes secondary high-current pulse trains from dedicated ultra-capacitor banks to the high-resistance NiTiNOL hinge cores, bypassing primary thermal dissipation rails:

$$Q_{\text{pulse}} = \int_0^{\Delta t_{\text{pulse}}} I_2^2(t) R_{\text{SMA}}(T) \, dt = m c_p \Delta T_{\text{overcome}} + Q_{\text{latent}}$$

The high $dI/dt$ instantaneous dissipation elevates the cold spot boundary past $A_f$ in under 120 ms, generating an additional $\Delta F = +35\text{ N}$ of impulsive recovery force, driving mechanical detents past spring-loaded latching pawls.

$$\Delta F_{\text{impulse}} = \Omega_A \cdot A_{\text{wire}} \cdot \Delta \xi_{\text{forced}} \ge F_{\text{friction\_max}} + F_{\text{spring\_preload}}$$
Pulse architecture: Gallium Nitride (GaN) HEMT switches rated for 60 V, 40 A at radiation tolerance > 100 krad(Si).
